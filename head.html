Consuma internal reference

What is actually inconsistent, with real examples

Every quote on this page is copied word for word from Consuma's live website, LinkedIn page, or the careers document you shared. Nothing here is made up or guessed at.

01

Consuma describes itself three different ways

  • Website titleConsuma. Rapid Research Platform.
  • LinkedInAn AI based market intelligence platform that generates insights and identifies trends from across the web.
  • Careers pageThe Intelligence Layer for all Brands.
02

The turnaround time claim does not agree with itself

  • Website FAQThis data is formatted and presented on your dashboard in the form of a report, all in the span of 30 Minutes.
  • Careers pageOur AI native Market Intelligence Platform transforms billions of real consumer signals into structured, decision ready intelligence. In seconds.
  • LinkedIn, post oneUse the Consuma cutting edge AI Engine to get research reports in just minutes.
  • LinkedIn, post twoMarket Research in just seconds, using the Consuma AI Engine.
03

The main call to action changes wording five times, all on the same website

  • Hero section buttonBook a Free Demo
  • Closing section buttonBook a Demo
  • Top navigationContact Us
  • FAQ buttonGet in touch
  • FAQ description text, right next to that same buttonIf you don't find an answer to your question, book a discovery call and our team will get in touch with you.
04

The careers page uses a completely different kind of call to action

  • Careers pageInterested? Fill up this form: forms.gle/z8KQwSXVC3mTmXkTA

It never uses Book a Demo, Get in touch, or Contact Us, the three phrases used everywhere else.

05

The product's own components only get named on the website

  • WebsiteSmart Scrapers. Autonomous Agents (AA). Fine Tuned Gen AI.
  • LinkedInConsuma cutting edge AI Engine
  • Careers pagethe AI layer powering the next generation of consumer brands

Smart Scrapers, Autonomous Agents, and AA never appear on LinkedIn or the careers page.

06

The tone is not the same on any two channels

  • LinkedIn🚀 As consumer behavior evolves online, so should your marketing strategies. 🌐💡 With Consuma.ai, dive into the sea of consumer data, surfacing instant and profound insights to elevate your marketing game! 📊✨
  • Website87% Proven Accuracy. Get precise with insights derived from data with a proven 87% industry leading accuracy vs secondary research.
  • Careers pageWe are not another dashboard. We are not another insights agency.

  • LinkedIn, used as decoration🚀🌐💡✨📊

  • LinkedIn, post one#Consuma #MarketInsights #BusinessIntelligence #DataDrivenSuccess

  • Website, Depth feature cardTrained on 5B datapoints

  • Website91% reduction in analyst hours

12

The 87 percent accuracy stat only exists on the website

  • Website87% accuracy vs secondary research

It is treated as a headline proof point on the site, but it never appears on LinkedIn or the careers page, even though both make other confidence claims.
