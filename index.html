Consuma / Internal Tool

Consuma Style Console

Generates on-brand copy for a given channel from a fixed, explicit rulebook. The rules are hardcoded below — the model fills in your brief, it does not decide the standard. Every output is auto-audited against the same rules.

Position (say the job, not the category)

POSITION
"Consuma replaces weeks of market research with a report the AI writes from the live internet, in under 30 minutes." This is the anchor sentence. Not "AI layer," not "intelligence layer," not "operating system for X" — those are category claims a competitor can copy-paste. This one is checkable.
BELIEF
"By the time traditional research lands, the market's already moved." The one opinion Consuma is allowed to repeat across channels. Everything else should sound like it's in service of this belief, not restating the company description.
NAME
ConsumaNever "Consuma AI Engine," "Consuma.ai" mid-sentence, or "the Consuma platform." Just Consuma.
TERMS
Smart Scrapers, Autonomous Agents (first use spells out, then "AA"). No other names for the same components.

Proof over adjective

RULE
Any claim about the team, the product, or the ambition needs a checkable anchor attached — a client name, a locked stat, or one concrete, specific detail. An adjective alone is not a claim.
APPROVED CLIENTS
Foxtale, Mosaic Wellness, DNA Consult, Empirical Media — the only names cleared to cite as proof.
FLAGGED
"high talent density," "zero bureaucracy," "real ownership," "exceptional folks," "world-class," "best-in-class" — every seed-stage careers page says these. Say what the person will actually build instead.

Locked numbers

STAT
87% accuracyvs. secondary research — always this phrasing.
STAT
91% reductionin analyst hours.
STAT
Under 30 minutesnever "30 mins," "30 Minutes," or a range.
STAT
5B+ datapointsnever "5M–10B" or restated ranges.
RULE
Digits always. Never invent a number not on this list.

The patterns that make copy smell AI-written

BANNED
"We are not X. We are not Y. We are Z." — the rule-of-three negation. It's the most recognizable startup-deck pattern there is; using it is what makes copy sound like it wrote itself.
BANNED
"isn't just about X, it's Y" / "beyond just X" constructions.
BANNED
"operating system for X," "intelligence layer," "unlock," "revolutionize," "game-changing," "unparalleled," "cutting-edge," "seamless."
BANNED
Em dashes, and spaced hyphens used as punctuation, banned entirely. Use a period or comma instead. (Compound words like "AI-powered" are fine — the ban is on the dash as a pause, not the hyphen as a word-joiner.)
BANNED VOCAB
delve, leverage, robust, landscape, realm, foster, elevate, streamline, empower, holistic, synergy, myriad, plethora, showcase, meticulous, utilize, harness, "in today's fast-paced world," "moreover/furthermore" as a sentence opener — the reflexive LinkedIn-thought-leader vocabulary. Full list lives in the tool's rule object.

Mechanics

EMOJI
Decorative emoji (🚀🌐💡✨ used for hype) banned everywhere. Functional emoji-as-icon in a structured doc (📍 location, ✉️ email in a careers page's contact line) is fine, max once per line, never repeated for emphasis.
CASE
Sentence case for headlines and subject lines. No Title Case, no ALL CAPS.
CTA
"Book a demo" or "Get in touch"— only these two, nothing else.
